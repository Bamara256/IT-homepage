Welcome to Our Department

The Department of Information Technology at ISBAT University prepares students for careers in software development, networking, databases and systems administration. Our courses combine theory with hands-on laboratory practice.

Our lecturers support every student to build practical, job-ready skills, so that graduates can compete confidently in Uganda, across East Africa and beyond.

Programmes Offered

  • Diploma in Information Technology
  • Bachelor of Information Technology
  • Bachelor of Computer Science
  • Master of Science in Information Technology
